About Sand & Aggregates

River Sand (Reti)

River sand is the standard choice for masonry work — laying bricks and blocks — where its coarser grain gives a stronger bond in mortar. We supply river sand for brickwork and general construction across Indore.

Plaster Sand

Plaster sand is finer and screened for wall plastering and finishing coats, where a smoother, more consistent texture is needed than river sand provides. We supply plaster sand separately from masonry sand for this reason.

Filling Sand

Filling sand is used for backfilling, levelling and base preparation ahead of flooring or foundation work, where fine grading and finish quality matter less than bulk and compaction.

Aggregates (Gitti)

Gravel and stone aggregate (gitti) are used in concrete mixes for foundations, slabs, columns, and roads, where the aggregate size affects the strength and finish of the concrete. 10mm suits slabs and thin sections, 20mm suits RCC columns, beams and foundation work, and 40mm suits foundation filling and heavy concrete work. We supply aggregate for both structural concrete and general fill work across Indore.

Stone Dust (Churi)

Stone dust (churi) is used for masonry, plastering, and as a base layer under pavers, where a fine, well-compacting material is needed.

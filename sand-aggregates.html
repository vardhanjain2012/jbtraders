Sand & Aggregate Supplier in Indore

River sand, plaster sand, gravel, and stone aggregate for masonry, plastering, and concrete work across Indore.

River Sand

River sand is the standard choice for masonry work — laying bricks and blocks — where its coarser grain gives a stronger bond in mortar. We supply river sand for brickwork and general construction across Indore.

Plaster Sand

Plaster sand is finer and screened for wall plastering and finishing coats, where a smoother, more consistent texture is needed than river sand provides. We supply plaster sand separately from masonry sand for this reason.

Gravel & Stone Aggregate

Gravel and stone aggregate (gitti) are used in concrete mixes for foundations, slabs, columns, and roads, where the aggregate size affects the strength and finish of the concrete. We supply aggregate for both structural concrete and general fill work across Indore.

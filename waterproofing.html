About Waterproofing

Waterproofing Compounds

Waterproofing compounds are mixed into or applied over concrete and plaster to stop water seepage on roofs, terraces, bathrooms, and external walls — the areas most prone to leaks and dampness in Indore's monsoon season. We supply waterproofing compounds for both new construction and repair of existing seepage problems.

UltraTech Waterproofing Tape

Waterproofing tape is used to seal joints, cracks, and pipe penetrations where liquid compounds alone don't give a reliable seal — a common fix for leakage around bathroom fittings and roof joints. We stock UltraTech waterproofing tape for these repair jobs.

Delivery Across Indore

Waterproofing compounds and tape are available for pickup or delivery to your site or home within Indore. Call or WhatsApp us with details of the area you need to waterproof so we can help you figure out what you need. See our FAQ page for details on order quantities and delivery timelines.

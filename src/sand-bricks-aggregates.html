Sizes and varieties

Reti — river and crushed sand — is available in grades suited to masonry, concrete and plastering work. Coarser sand is used for brickwork and concrete mixes, while finer sand is used for plastering and finishing coats, so the grade of sand you need depends on the stage your site is at.

Gitti, the stone aggregate used in concrete, is available in the standard sizes used across construction in Indore — smaller aggregate for slab concreting and RCC columns and beams, larger aggregate for foundations and mass concrete work. Tell us the mix you are working to and we will advise the aggregate size to order.

Churi, or stone dust, is the fine crushed material used for masonry and plastering mortar and as a compacted base layer under pavers. It is supplied by trolley or truck load in the same way as sand and aggregate.

Bricks are supplied in two sizes. Laal eent, also called gutka eent, measures 8 × 4 × 4 inches and is used for load-bearing and main walls. Patli eent measures 9 × 4.5 × 1.5 inches and is used for partition walls, boundary walls and lighter construction where full load-bearing strength isn't required.

About Sand, Bricks & Aggregates

Reti (Sand)

Reti is used for masonry, plastering and general concrete work — its grain is graded to suit brickwork as well as finishing coats. We supply reti for both structural and finishing work.

Gitti (Stone Aggregate)

Gitti is used in concrete mixes for foundations, slabs, columns, and roads, where the aggregate size affects the strength and finish of the concrete. It's available in the standard sizes used for slab concreting as well as RCC columns, beams and foundation work. We supply gitti for both structural concrete and general fill work.

Churi (Stone Dust)

Churi is used for masonry, plastering, and as a base layer under pavers, where a fine, well-compacting material is needed.

Laal Eent (Gutka)

Laal eent, also called gutka eent, is used for load-bearing and main structural walls, valued for its strength and thermal properties compared to some alternative block materials. We supply laal eent for new construction as well as smaller repair and extension work.

Patli Eent

Patli eent is a thinner brick used for partition walls, boundary work, and lighter construction where full load-bearing strength isn't required.

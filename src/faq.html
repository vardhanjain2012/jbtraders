Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering building materials from J.B. Traders in Indore. For anything not covered here, call or WhatsApp us directly.

What does J.B. Traders sell?

Cement, tile adhesives, epoxy grouts, tile fillers, cover blocks, waterproofing compounds and wall putty are our main lines. We also supply reti (sand), gitti (aggregate), churi (stone dust) and eent (bricks).

Which brands does J.B. Traders stock?

Cement — UltraTech, JK Super, Wonder Cement, Nuvoco, JK Lakshmi, Mangalam Birla Uttam, JK White and Birla White, plus Birla Wall Care Putty. Tile adhesives and grouts — Kerakoll, Roff, Tenax, Mapei and Araldite. Other brands arranged on request.

Which areas does J.B. Traders deliver to?

All areas of Indore, plus Dewas, Sanwer, Rau, Pithampur and Dr. Ambedkar Nagar (Mhow).

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No minimum order. We supply single bags as well as bulk truck-load quantities.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI and net banking.

Do you supply in bulk for contractors and builders?

Yes. Contact us with your requirement and we will quote for bulk quantities.

What are your delivery timelines?

Most orders are delivered within one day.

How long has J.B. Traders been in business?

Since 6 October 1992 — over thirty years supplying building materials in Indore. We deal transparently on quantity, quality and price.

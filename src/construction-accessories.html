About Construction Accessories

UltraTech Super Adhesive Tape

UltraTech Super Adhesive Tape is a BOPP-based adhesive film used for sealing joints between formwork panels, covering plywood edges, and preventing cement slurry leakage during concrete pouring. We supply it for both new construction and shuttering repair work.

Concrete Cover Blocks

Cover blocks are small precast concrete spacers placed under and around rebar before a concrete pour, keeping the reinforcement at the correct distance from the shuttering so it ends up fully embedded within the specified concrete cover. They're used in footings, slabs, beams, and columns on any RCC structure, and are available in standard thicknesses. We supply cover blocks to contractors and builders across Indore.

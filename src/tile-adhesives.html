About Tile Adhesives

Tile Adhesives — Kerakoll, Roff & Tenax

Tile adhesive replaces traditional cement-sand mortar for fixing tiles, giving a stronger bond, less shrinkage, and faster laying — especially important for large-format vitrified tiles that don't hold well with mortar alone. We stock adhesives from Kerakoll, Roff, Tenax and Mapei, brands widely used by tilers and contractors in Indore for both wall and floor applications.

Where Tile Adhesive Is Used

Vitrified floor tiles, ceramic wall tiles in kitchens and bathrooms, and exterior tiling on facades or terraces all typically call for a dedicated adhesive rather than plain mortar, particularly over existing tiled or smooth concrete surfaces. Contractors across Indore use these adhesives on both new construction and renovation work.

Epoxy Grouts & Joint Fillers

Once tiles are laid, epoxy grout and tile joint fillers seal the gaps between them — epoxy grout in particular resists staining and water penetration better than standard cement grout, which matters in kitchens, bathrooms, and high-traffic flooring. We also supply epoxy fillers used for marble and granite surfaces.

Marble, granite and natural stone

We supply complete solutions for marble, granite and natural stone — adhesives for fixing stone on floors and walls, Tenax epoxy fillers for chips, cracks and repair work, and Kerakoll Superflex for water-sensitive stone that stains with ordinary adhesive. Available at our Nemi Nagar shop with delivery across Indore, Dewas, Sanwer, Rau, Pithampur and Dr. Ambedkar Nagar.

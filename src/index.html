About Our Products

Cement

We supply all major cement brands — UltraTech, JK Super, Wonder Cement, Nuvoco, JK Lakshmi, Mangalam Birla Uttam, JK White and Birla White — in OPC, PPC and white cement, for residential and commercial sites across Indore. All types and grades available, and other brands arranged on request. Tell us what your site needs.

Tile Adhesives

Kerakoll, Roff, Mapei and MYK Laticrete adhesives for vitrified tiles, wall and floor tiles, marble and natural stone. Chosen by contractors and homeowners in Indore who need a strong, long-lasting bond without traditional cement mortar.

Grouts & Fillers

Kerakoll Fugalite epoxy grout, Tenax epoxy fillers and tile joint fillers for sealing gaps between tiles and repairing marble and granite surfaces. Used across Indore for both new installations and repair work on existing flooring.

Sand, Bricks & Aggregates

Reti for masonry and plastering, gitti and churi for concrete work and paver base, and laal eent (gutka) and patli eent red bricks for load-bearing, partition and boundary walls. Supplied to residential and commercial sites across Indore.

Waterproofing

Waterproofing compounds for roofs, terraces, and bathrooms. Used across Indore to prevent seepage before and after construction.

Construction Accessories

UltraTech Super Adhesive Tape for sealing formwork joints and plywood edges, plus concrete cover blocks for maintaining rebar spacing in RCC slabs and columns. Supplied across Indore for both new construction and site repair work.

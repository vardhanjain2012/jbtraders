About Bricks & Blocks

Red Bricks (Laal Eent)

Red bricks remain the standard walling material for both load-bearing and partition walls in Indore, valued for their strength and thermal properties compared to some alternative block materials. We supply red bricks for new construction as well as smaller repair and extension work.

Moti eent (large bricks) are used for load-bearing walls and main structural work. Patli eent (thin bricks) are used for partition walls, boundary work, and lighter construction.

Concrete Cover Blocks

Cover blocks are small precast concrete spacers placed under and around rebar before a concrete pour, keeping the reinforcement at the correct distance from the shuttering so it ends up fully embedded within the specified concrete cover. They're used in footings, slabs, beams, and columns on any RCC structure, and are available in standard thicknesses. We supply cover blocks to contractors and builders across Indore.

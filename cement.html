UltraTech OPC 53 Grade

Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) 53 Grade is the standard choice for RCC work — foundations, columns, beams, and slabs — where higher early and final strength matters. We supply UltraTech OPC 53 Grade to contractors and homeowners building across Indore, from individual houses to larger commercial sites.

UltraTech PPC (Portland Pozzolana Cement)

PPC is widely used for plastering, masonry, and general construction where a slower heat of hydration and better workability are preferred over the higher early strength of OPC. It's a common choice for boundary walls, plastering work, and non-structural elements. We keep UltraTech PPC available for site delivery within Indore.

White Cement

White cement is used for finishing work — wall putty base coats, marble and tile joint filling, decorative finishes, and repair patches where a smooth, non-grey surface is needed. We supply white cement in smaller quantities suited to finishing and touch-up jobs across Indore.

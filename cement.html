About Cement

Grey Cement

UltraTech — OPC 53 Grade is used for RCC work — foundations, columns, beams and slabs — where higher early and final strength is needed. PPC offers better workability and is commonly used for plastering, masonry and general construction. Both available for site delivery across Indore.

JK Super — Suited to general construction, plastering and masonry work across residential and commercial sites in Indore.

Ambuja — Used for RCC work, brickwork and plastering across residential and commercial construction sites in Indore.

White Cement

Birla White — Used for finishing work, repair patches and marble or tile joint filling where a smooth, non-grey surface is needed.

JK White — Suited to wall finishing and decorative work where a clean white base is required.

Wall Putty

Birla Wall Care Putty — Applied before painting to fill minor cracks and even out the wall surface, indoors and outdoors, giving a smooth base coat for paint.
